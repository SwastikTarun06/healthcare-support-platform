Emergency Checklist

When to call 999

  • Unconsciousness or not breathing
  • Severe chest pain
  • Severe bleeding that won't stop

When to go to A&E

  • Major injuries, suspected broken bones
  • Severe head injury or suspected stroke

When a clinic is enough

  • Minor wounds, infections, colds, vaccinations
  • Medication refills and routine checks
